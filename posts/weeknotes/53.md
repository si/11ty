## OKR Daily Card is live

The side project I've been poking at for a few weeks now has a real URL: **[okr.sijobling.com](https://okr.sijobling.com/)**, running on Railway.

It started as a single-user demo for tracking quarterly OKRs, a mission per objective, a few daily actions to tick off, key results they roll up to. Every tick asks "what did you actually do?" so the history is real evidence rather than a bare streak count. Over the last couple of weeks it's turned into something closer to a proper (if still very personal) app: magic-link sign-in instead of a password, a guided onboarding flow that turns your own answers into your own objectives and daily actions rather than my invented demo data, a weekly view with a "copy week summary" button for pasting into a check-in doc, and an MCP hook-up so Claude can check in on it directly. Ten stories in, all done. Nothing fancy, just useful.

## Building up to 50km

Two weeks today I'm walking the **[Ultra Challenge Kent](https://www.justgiving.com/page/si-kent-50?utm_medium=FR&utm_source=CL)**, 50km from 7:30am with a few checkpoints along the way, raising money for **Dementia UK**. Both my dad and my gran live with dementia, so this one's personal.

Pulled my Strava log to see where training actually stands: this week I did 36.1km across 11 sessions (roughly 7hrs 47min), against 18.6km the week before, basically doubled it. Year to date I'm at 911.4km, already past all of 2024's total with three months still to go.

The number that actually matters, though, is the longest single session, a 31.1km walk back in May, plus a couple more in the 20-25km range since. The most recent proper long one was 25km around the Peak District in July, and that was tiring, so I'm going in eyes open that Kent will be double that distance. Last time I attempted 50km in one go was London to Brighton back in 2018, and I'm eight years older than I was then. Feeling ready regardless. If you fancy chipping in for Dementia UK, [the page is here](https://www.justgiving.com/page/si-kent-50?utm_medium=FR&utm_source=CL).
