**OKR habits prototypes.** Something I haven't mentioned yet is all the work I've been doing on the OKR habits prototypes. This week I started exploring a new visualisation for OKRs, to show progress and regular check-ins. Each OKR gets a ring, and each marker around it is a check-in point, coloured by the status I gave at the time (on track, at risk, blocked or complete). Weekly, daily and monthly OKRs all work the same way, so you can see at a glance whether the habit of checking in is holding up. Tapping the centre flips the ring to show the key results, and tapping a marker opens a check-in with a status and a short note. It's a mockup with made-up data for now.
