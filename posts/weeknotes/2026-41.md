## 50km Ultra Challenge

The week was all about the [Ultra Challenge Kent](https://www.justgiving.com/page/si-kent-50?utm_medium=FR&utm_source=CL), 50km for Dementia UK on Saturday. Fitness before it was mostly warming up, with calmer sessions focused on endurance and calves.

The walk itself was exhilarating. There's the camaraderie we've come to expect from the Ultra events now, and the weather was perfect, dry and mild. The first 12km went really fast, then we refreshed at the rest stop and blasted through to halfway for lunch. After recharging on the "meal deal" and a change of socks, we carried on through the harder stretch to the final rest stop, where I topped up on caffeine and sweets and put my new head torch on for the final 11km.

That last section was hard, especially once it went dark and we hit a steep incline for the final 2km. We got together to run over the finishing line though. I'm really happy with our time of 9h43, but then we saw the leaderboard and realised we were in the last 100 of 700, so it's time to work on our pace.
