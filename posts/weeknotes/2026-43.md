## PETALS

**Blog.** Sertan's new article, [Why PETALS asks how people feel as AI changes our work](https://usepetals.com/blog/why-petals-asks-how-people-feel/), went live on Friday. It looks at what recent research from METR and DORA says about AI and software development, from the gap between how fast work feels and the value it delivers to the burnout and friction that delivery metrics miss, and ties each finding to one of the five petals: Productivity, Enjoyment, Serenity, Learning and Teamwork. It lands on why it's still worth asking how work feels as the tools change, and leaving room at the next retro to talk about the scores.

![Two cats on a whitewashed wall at sunset, the featured image for the article on how AI changes our work](/img/assets/2026/10/petals-blog-ai-feelings-hero.webp)


**Slack integration.** The app didn't get much attention this week, but Brian has been working on the Slack integration, and I've been testing it and feeding back to him. This is the happy path journey: connect a Slack workspace to your PETALS organisation, choose the channel each team's notifications post to, and use the nudge button on a snapshot to prompt the team in that channel. It's behind a feature switch, so we can push it to production and work with real world data. Here it is in its current, basic form.
