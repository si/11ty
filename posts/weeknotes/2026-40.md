## PETALS

Mostly review and testing this week rather than building.

- Tested Brian's "Chart 10" PR locally with 11 team snapshots on a free plan. The last-5 limit on the team view and last-10 on "my snapshots" both worked, but I found a cheeky route past it: click the oldest flower on the far right, then keep following the "last time" flower further back. Asked for that link to be removed on the oldest allowed view, and the PR was merged on Thursday.

- The cookie consent banner for analytics and chat tracking is still open and waiting on a decision.
- Documented the PR conventions (project, milestone and labels) in the marketing site's `CLAUDE.md`, so they stop getting forgotten.

## OKR Daily Card: edit outcomes and add actions

Two gaps in the side project closed this week. You can now **edit an outcome** after creating it (title, key results, tasks and the completion timeline), and **add a daily action** to an existing outcome without starting over. Both sit behind ownership checks so you can only touch your own, and both have unit tests. [okr.sijobling.com](https://okr.sijobling.com/) has it live.
