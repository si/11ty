## PETALS

Mostly reviewing and testing this week rather than building.

Brian's "Chart 10" PR limits how many snapshots free plans can look back at, so I tested it locally with 11 team snapshots. The limits held, but I found a cheeky way round them: click the oldest flower on the far right, then keep following the "last time" flower further back in time. I asked for that link to be removed on the oldest allowed view, and the PR was merged on Thursday.

Warp also landed two changes, copyable snapshot results and caching the report petal averages per instance. The cookie consent banner PR is still open, waiting on a decision.

I also wrote our PR conventions (project, milestone and labels) into the marketing site's `CLAUDE.md`, so anything I point at that repo opens PRs the way I like them.

## OKR Daily Card

![OKR Daily Card desktop view](/img/assets/2026/09/okr-daily-card-desktop.png)

Two gaps in [OKR Daily Card](https://okr.sijobling.com/) got closed this week. You can now **edit an outcome** after creating it (title, key results, tasks and completion timeline), and **add a daily action** to an existing outcome without starting it again from scratch.

Both check you own the objective before changing anything, and both have unit tests. Jules did the build, I reviewed it and merged it.
