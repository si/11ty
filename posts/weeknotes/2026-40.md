## OKR Daily Card: edit outcomes and add actions

Two gaps in the side project closed this week. You can now **edit an outcome** after creating it (title, key results, tasks and the completion timeline), and **add a daily action** to an existing outcome without starting over. Both sit behind ownership checks so you can only touch your own, and both have unit tests. [okr.sijobling.com](https://okr.sijobling.com/) has it live.
